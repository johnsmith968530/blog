# Trip to Berlin

## LAS -> FRA
* The Condor check-in area (LAS Terminal 3, second floor) was invisible (no sign or anything) until around 4 hours before takeoff time. It was near door 45, where the airlines to Mexico are.
* I found the Condor check-in area using the info terminal by searching for "Condor".
* Lots of electrical outlets in the D-gate departure area, much more than the domestic gates, I think.
* I plugged in the USB-C cord of my MacBook Air (h353) into the USB-C outlet in the seat back in front of me, and I seem to have power.
* Looks like Condor charges for the wired headphones that plug into the seat back. They offer WiFi, movies, TV shows, etc.
