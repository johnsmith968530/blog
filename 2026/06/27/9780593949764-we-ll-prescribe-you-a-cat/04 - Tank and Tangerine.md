# 4. Tank and Tangerine

**Source track:** Track 007

## Characters

- **Tomoka Takamine** — a 32-year-old handbag designer/shop owner; talented, rigidly perfectionist, a neat-freak.
- **Junko** — her business partner, who handles the business/accounting side.
- **Mitsuki** — the senior design assistant.
- **Daigo** — Tomoka's boyfriend of five years; a talented chef who drifts from job to job and won't commit.
- **Dr. Nikke** — the clinic doctor (encountered drunk on catnip tea).
- **Chitose** — the nurse.
- **Tangerine** — first prescribed cat: a female ragdoll, four years old, blue-eyed, docile and clingy; "works" at a cat café. (Misprescribed.)
- **Tank** — second prescribed cat: a male American Shorthair, light grey with black stripes, yellow-brown eyes, an energetic "night-zoomies" tornado; lives in a mansion with other cats.
- **Abino** — a geiko customer (protagonist of Story 5).
- **Kozue** — a manicurist/customer who connects Abino to Tomoka.
- **Dr. Suda ("Dr. Kokoro")** — the elderly vet at Suda Animal Hospital.

## Summary

Three assistants quit at once, fed up with Tomoka's impossible perfectionism. Partner **Junko** urges her to see "an interesting doctor" she's heard about through the Gion grapevine. Furious but cornered, Tomoka visits the clinic, where Dr. Nikke — hiccupping on catnip tea, with a nurse who drinks the tea herself — tells her bluntly that she's "doing everything the wrong way" and prescribes "a stronger cat" for two weeks. He hands over a cat he calls "Tank."

At home the cat turns out to be a gorgeous long-haired white **ragdoll** with blue eyes — calm, plush, irresistible. Tomoka and boyfriend **Daigo** keep him, and Tomoka becomes obsessed: she photographs him endlessly and is mesmerized by his gummy, silicone-like paw pads. She's inspired to design cat-themed merchandise. Meanwhile, Daigo has **quit his chef job yet again**, and Tomoka (32, wanting marriage) increasingly feels the relationship has stagnated.

Customer **Kozue** wants bags with a cat illustration; Junko and the returning Mitsuki embrace it. When Tomoka goes back to the clinic to extend keeping "Tank" as a design model, the doctor cheerfully confesses a mistake: **he prescribed the wrong cat** — the ragdoll is actually **Tangerine** (female, four, clingy, works at a cat café). He now prescribes the real **Tank**, a male American Shorthair, on top of Tangerine, warning her to "finish both completely" or they'll lose their effect.

Tank arrives as pure chaos — the **"Night Zoomies"** — ricocheting off walls, toppling everything, dragging the normally placid Tangerine into the mayhem. Daigo is useless at catching him; Tomoka ends up scratched. Yet she's charmed, and the two cats' contrasting looks (Tangerine's dark ears; Tank's stripes; both with marble-like eyes) fuse into a successful new design theme — "sophisticated sweetness for adults."

The story braids into Story 5: **Abino**, a geiko, comes to Tomoka's shop to collect an orange bag and is moved to tears by a gold cat-face charm Tomoka gives her, confessing her own cat **Chitose** has been missing for a year. Tomoka — who has surprised herself by grieving cats she had for only a month — consoles her: *"less time doesn't mean less love… there are those who are irreplaceable,"* and urges her to return to the clinic, saying, *"if you truly want the door to open, it will."* The two women go to the Nakagyo building but **cannot find the alley**; they locate the building on Fuyacho Street, only to find the fifth-floor unit locked and apparently empty. The shady neighbor (Akira Shina) warns them the unit is **jinxed and vacant**. Abino says it's where Chitose was found.

That night the cats get into a houseplant, and Tomoka rushes them in the middle of the night to **Suda Animal Hospital**, where the elderly **Dr. Suda** kindly saves them despite the hour. Tomoka realizes that responsibly keeping cats means real preparation — knowing the vet, emergency hours, and more. She finally confronts Daigo: he's too old to keep quitting, won't meet her parents, has no plans — *"Get your act together. I'm done."* Daigo deflects, saying he'll tell her something "once he finds a new job."

Tomoka returns both cats to the clinic. Dr. Nikke, now sober and gentle, says the feeling of not wanting to let go of something warm will stay with her. Tangerine goes back to her cat-café job; Tank to his mansion. The doctor reveals the clinic "borrowed the name" from a hospital that saved their lives — **Dr. Kokoro / Suda Animal Hospital** — and his smile "resembled Dr. Kokorosuda." Tomoka leaves; the alley is gone, only the tall Nakagyo building remaining. She resolves to have an honest talk with Daigo and, once her life is steadier, perhaps get a cat of her own.

## The cat's role

The two misprescribed cats — placid Tangerine and manic Tank — crack open Tomoka's rigidity, inspire her creative breakthrough, and teach her that real care (for cats, for a partner, for herself) means choosing responsibility over perfection. They also become the bridge that connects her to Abino.
