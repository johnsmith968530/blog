# 5. Mimita

**Source tracks:** Track 008 – Track 009

## Characters

- **Abino** (real name Amitakura/Takeda) — a 26-year-old geiko in Gion; lives at the Okiya Komanoya.
- **Shizue** — the proprietress of the okiya.
- **Yuriha** — a sister geisha at the okiya.
- **Ioka** — a wealthy Kyoto building owner and Abino's generous regular client.
- **Dr. Suda ("Dr. Kokoro")** — the elderly veterinarian at Suda Animal Hospital.
- **Kajiwara** — a young man (the cat-shelter deputy director from Story 2) who adopts the black cat Nike.
- **Chitose** — Abino's beloved calico cat, rescued from an illegal breeder; and the name of the clinic's nurse (the two are mystically one).
- **Dr. Nikke** — the clinic doctor; a man who himself adopted Nike, a black cat rescued from the same fifth-floor room.
- **Mimita** — the prescribed cat: a male five-month-old Scottish Fold, round light-brown face, ears "like ribbons."
- **Tomoka** — the designer from Story 4, who befriends Abino.

## Summary

At a Gion dinner hosted by client **Ioka**, geiko **Abino** meets **Dr. Suda**, the veterinarian. Ioka recounts how a tenant in his Nakagyo building — an illegal cat breeder — skipped town, leaving cats behind; Dr. Suda and volunteers rescued them (only two survived), cleaned the unit, and held a memorial. Abino is quietly heartbroken.

Abino's own story: she left her hometown after middle school, trained at the Okiya Komanoya, and debuted as a geiko in Gion. She adopted one of those rescued cats — a scruffy, kidney-damaged calico she named **Chitose** — and devoted herself to her frequent vet care. Chitose grew healthy but shy and haughty, and the two bonded deeply. When Chitose's kidneys failed, Dr. Suda offered a referral to an advanced hospital in the East; Abino vowed to do anything. That night Chitose was unusually affectionate, licking Abino's cheek and sleeping on her pillow.

Then **Chitose escaped** through a window Abino thought she'd latched — and was never found. Abino searched the streets on her hands and knees until morning, posted flyers across Kyoto, Shiga, and Osaka, and sank into guilt and grief that threatened her work (a geiko must not smudge her white makeup with tears). She even visited the empty breeding unit seeking closure; Dr. Suda warned her only resentment lingered there. A year on, she still couldn't move on.

This is where Story 4 crosses over: at designer **Tomoka**'s shop, picking up a bag, Abino breaks down over a gold cat-face charm and confesses everything about Chitose. Tomoka, who grieved cats she'd had only a month, tells her *"less time doesn't mean less love… there are those who are irreplaceable,"* and urges her to return to the clinic — *"if you truly want the door to open, it will."*

Abino returns to the building and, for the first time, has the courage to turn the knob. The once-empty unit is now a bright clinic. The nurse — **Chitose** — greets her by name: *"Miss Amitakura, we've been waiting for you."* The doctor is **Dr. Nikke**, the same man Abino had seen at Suda Animal Hospital who adopted the black cat **Nike** (rescued from this very room). When Abino murmurs, *"My cat won't come home,"* he says, *"We'll prescribe you a cat,"* and brings out **Mimita** — a five-month-old Scottish Fold with a round face and ears like crushed ribbons, sociable but wary, prone to running if pursued, and required to sleep in the patient's room. Ten-day course; if she improves, she need not return.

At the okiya, Shizue and Yuriha adore Mimita instantly, but **Abino holds back**, guilt-stricken that loving another cat means betraying Chitose. She rebuffs Mimita even when he approaches her. Then **Mimita escapes** — through the same kind of faulty window latch that let Chitose out: a dislodged rain gutter had pushed the frame so the "locked" crescent latch no longer caught. Abino chases him into the wet night street. On her knees on the cobblestones, she finally breaks: she apologizes to Mimita for her coldness, admitting she was terrified that liking him meant forgetting Chitose. *"Come back, my little cat."* Mimita licks her fingers and lets her lift him up.

Back inside, they discover the broken latch — and Abino realizes **Chitose's escape wasn't (only) her fault**, easing her crushing guilt. She decides to keep Mimita.

Returning to the clinic, Abino meets the nurse's parting gift of words. **Chitose the nurse speaks as the cat**: *"I didn't disappear to make things difficult for you. I made the decision and left of my own volition… forget about me and go pick him up."* Dr. Nikke tells Abino that cats have their own worlds and look toward the future; you can't release the cat's tail because you miss her, and she can't shake you off because *"even now, she still loves you"* — but *"it might be time to let go and bid her goodbye."* Abino closes her eyes and internally says goodbye to Chitose: *thank you, I'm sorry I couldn't protect you, I love you, goodbye.* As she leaves, the nurse adds, *"You said forever and ever. I'm sorry I couldn't be with you forever and ever. Take care. Bye."*

Abino steps into the blue sky and phones a friend: **Mimita will be "at a shopping mall in Kusatsu"** — like all the therapy cats, he returns to his own life/job — and she heads there to see him, no longer lost, looking toward the future.

The book closes with a quiet coda: Dr. Nikke alone in the examination room, musing that this is where he was "born and raised." Chitose scolds him for napping and relying on luck, and reminds him they're **"a bonded pair."** A new patient — a young woman who heard about them through the grapevine — arrives. Dr. Nikke smiles as always: *"We'll prescribe you a cat. Chitose, please bring me the cat."* The cycle continues.

## The cat's role

Mimita is prescribed to Abino not to replace Chitose but to release her: his escape re-stages Chitose's, this time with a rescue, letting Abino forgive herself, accept love again, and finally say goodbye. The story reveals the clinic's heart — Dr. Nikke and Chitose themselves are a rescued "bonded pair," and the therapy cats are how the dead/lost continue to return.
