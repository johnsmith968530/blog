# We'll Prescribe You a Cat — Story Summaries

*By Ishida Sho, translated by E. Madison Shimoda. Audiobook read by Naruto Komatsu and Natsumi Kuroda (Penguin Random House Audio).*

This audiobook is a collection of **five inter-related short stories**, all set in Kyoto and linked by a single mysterious premise. This folder contains one detailed, full-spoiler summary per story.

## The shared premise (read this first)

Every story revolves around the **Nakagyo Kokoro Clinic for the Soul**, a clinic on the fifth floor of a narrow, grimy building hidden down a dark alley in Nakagyo Ward, Kyoto. The building's address is given in the maddening Kyoto style — *"East of Takoyakushi Street, south of Tominokoji Street, west of Rokkaku Street, north of Fuyacho Street"* — and the alleyway seems to appear or vanish depending on the moment and on whether the seeker truly needs to find it. To outsiders the fifth-floor unit is officially **vacant and "jinxed"** (the neighbor in the unit next door swears it's been empty for years and is haunted by voices and cat meows).

Inside, a young, oddly cheerful doctor — **Dr. Nikke** — and a pale, curt nurse — **Chitose** — see walk-in patients. The clinic is not really a psychiatric practice; it "borrowed the name" from a real place that saved the doctor's life: **Suda Animal Hospital** (run by the elderly veterinarian **Dr. Suda**, a.k.a. "Dr. Kokoro"). Instead of medication, Dr. Nikke **prescribes a cat** for a limited "course" (usually ten days to two weeks), complete with an instruction leaflet (feeding, water, litter, and idiosyncratic behavioral notes). The therapy cats are not strays — they all "have jobs" and homes they return to when the prescription ends (a cat café, a mansion, a shopping mall, etc.). The doctor insists a cat will only truly help — and only be adoptable — when there is a genuine **"emotional connection."**

Across the five stories the same figures and motifs recur: Dr. Nikke, the nurse Chitose, Dr. Suda and Suda Animal Hospital, the "jinxed" fifth-floor unit, Kajiwara (the cat-shelter deputy director who looks just like Dr. Nikke), Akira Shina (the shady magnetic-necklace salesman who rents the unit next door), and a network of rescued cats (notably the black cat Nike and the calico Chitose) who connect the human characters in ways they only half-understand.
