# Roasted Salmon Belly
* Use one sheet of foil to cover the baking pan, and a second sheet that's been deliberately wrinkled so that the skin doesn't have a flat surface to stick to.
* Coat the wrinkled foil with coconut oil to further prevent sticking.
* Cook the salmon belly slices skin-side down.
* Coat the salmon belly with a bit of ponzu sauce to dissolve and trap the aromatic chemicals that cause the fishy odor.
* Cook at 425 for 15(?) minutes for thick slices, or maybe 10 minutes for thinner slices. Consider using the "broil" setting for the last 2-3 minutes for browning.
  * Watch that last minute carefully to avoid burning.
* Goes well with white rice and a pickled plum.
