The sequence you've listed:  
**0, 1, 4, 9, 16, ...**  

is the sequence of **perfect squares**.

Let's analyze it:

- $ 0^2 = 0 $  
- $ 1^2 = 1 $  
- $ 2^2 = 4 $  
- $ 3^2 = 9 $  
- $ 4^2 = 16 $  
- $ 5^2 = 25 $, and so on...

So the general term of this sequence is:  
$$
a_n = n^2
$$
where $ n $ starts from 0.

### Summary:
- **Type of sequence**: Perfect squares
- **Formula**: $ a_n = n^2 $
- **Next term after 16**: $ 5^2 = 25 $

✅ This sequence is a well-known arithmetic sequence of squares.
