# Butter Scallops with Mushrooms
* Great Value (Walmart) brand frozen scallops
* Great Value (Walmart) brand frozen mushrooms
* Put frozen scallops and mushrooms in small pot.
* Add vinegar (I used apple cider vinegar w/ the mother) and rice wine for cooking.
  * This combination helps eliminate the "fishy" odor from frozen seafood.
  * Mix well, breaking apart scallops and mushrooms that are stuck together. This helps make sure the vinegar and rice wine touch all surfaces.
  * Without draining, add cold water to cover.
  * Heat until simmering, stirring occasionally. Since the vinegar and rice wine are still in the liquid, it will absorb the fishy odors as the scallops thaw.
  * Drain well.
  * Add butter and heat again to cook a bit more.
  * Add lime or lemon juice for taste.
