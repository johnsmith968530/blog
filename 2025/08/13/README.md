# Fried Scallop Noodles with Mushroom and Ikura
* I made a variation of https://github.com/johnsmith968530/blog/blob/here-and-now/2025/08/05/butter_scallops_with_mushrooms.md except that I added a package of beef ramen noodles to the water after it started boiling. I drained the water after the noodles became al dente, then added butter and fried the noodles, scallops, and mushrooms together. After frying lightly for a few minutes, I added lemon juice and the ikura (salmon roe).
