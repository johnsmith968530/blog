# Genmaicha Fried Egg
* Use large pot, coconut oil with some sesame oil
* Fry the egg at 250+ degrees with cover
* Add genmaicha on top as a garnish

# Oyster w/ Ikura & Quail Egg
* Oyster on a half-shell
* Ikura
* Quail egg
* Ponzu sauce
* Lime / lemon juice (the ponzu sauce wasn't sour enough for my taste)
* Maybe add a seaweed garnish next time?
