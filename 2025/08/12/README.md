# Genmaicha Fried Egg
* Use large pot, coconut oil with some sesame oil
* Fry the egg at 250+ degrees with cover
* Add genmaicha on top as a garnish

# Oyster w/ Ikura & Quail Egg
* Oyster on a half-shell
  * I put the entire oysters w/ shells in a bowl of warm water with some vinegar first, to dissolve and rinse off some of that seafood smell and also to warm up the oysters just a bit.
  * There will be something like a tendon attaching the oyster to the shell. Sever this with a spoon so that the oyster can slide off the shell.
* Ikura
* Quail egg
  * The quail eggs I got tend to have a thick inner membrane that makes it somewhat more difficult to extract the contents as compared to a chicken egg. I had to cut this inner membrane with a knife, not just break the shell open like I would normally do with a chicken egg.
* Ponzu sauce
* Lime / lemon juice (the ponzu sauce wasn't sour enough for my taste)
* Maybe add a seaweed garnish next time? Diced shallots?
