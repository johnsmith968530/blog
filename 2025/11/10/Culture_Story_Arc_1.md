## First Prompt
```text
The following two quotes are in the preface of _Consider Phlebas_ by Iain M. Banks:

Idolatry is worse than carnage.
-- The Koran, 2: 190
Gentile or Jew
O you who turn the wheel and look to windward,
Consider Phlebas, who was once handsome and tall as you.
-- T. S. Eliot, “The Waste Land,” IV

Unpack the meanings / symbolism / connotations, especially in reference to the novel _Consider Phlebas_.
```

## Second Prompt
```text
Compare and contrast The Culture's views in _Consider Phlebas_, the first Culture novel, with their views in _The Hydrogen Sonata_, the last Culture novel. Treat The Culture as if it were a character progressing through a story arc that spans all the novels.
```
